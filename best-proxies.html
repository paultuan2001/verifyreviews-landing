Frequently Asked Questions

What are rotating residential proxies?

Rotating residential proxies assign a real IP address from a home user to your connection. Every request you make (or after a set time) changes your IP, making it look like different real internet users are browsing. This prevents scraping systems from detecting and blocking you.

Why does IPRoyal stand out?

Most proxy networks operate on a monthly subscription model, and any unused bandwidth expires at the end of the billing cycle. IPRoyal is one of the very few networks offering pay-as-you-go proxy bandwidth that never expires, making it exceptionally cost-effective.

Is running residential proxies compliant?

Yes, residential proxy networks utilize IP addresses voluntarily shared by participants in peer-to-peer networks (who are compensated). They are fully legal when used for legitimate business intelligence, SEO monitoring, or web data extraction.
