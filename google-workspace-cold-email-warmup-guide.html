Google Workspace Cold Email Guide: Safe Sending Limits & Warmup

Learn how to configure Google Workspace for cold outreach in 2026. Setup SPF, DKIM, DMARC, custom tracking domain, and safe daily email limits.

Editor's Direct Verdict (TL;DR)

Sending cold outreach directly from a primary Google Workspace domain risks permanent domain suspension. Best practice involves buying secondary domains, configuring SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and warming up each inbox for 14+ days using WarmupInbox before sending 30-50 cold emails daily.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does Google ban accounts for cold emailing?

A: Yes, if bounce rates exceed 2% or spam report rates exceed 0.1%, Google will suspend Workspace mailboxes.

Q: Should I use my primary company domain for cold email?

A: Never use your primary domain for cold outreach. Always purchase secondary domains to protect core email communications.
